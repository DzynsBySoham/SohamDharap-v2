How retrieval works

Each document becomes a TF‑IDF vector. Your query is ranked against the indexed documents using cosine similarity.

This is lexical NLP retrieval, not an LLM or semantic embedding model. It matches words, not hidden meaning. Scores aren’t probabilities. English stop words are removed.
